Privacy policy

C1 Speaking Scorer · Last updated 18 November 2026

C1 Speaking Scorer is a free web page that helps examiners score Cambridge C1 Advanced Speaking and save the results to their own spreadsheet. It has no server of its own: everything runs in your browser.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your scores (candidate name, date, examiner, band scores and comments) are saved only where you choose: a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive, or an Excel file on your device.
  • In your browser, the page keeps the candidate you are scoring as an unsaved draft, which spreadsheet or file you chose, and, where your browser can't save straight into files, a copy of your Excel workbook. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Google account access

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks only for the drive.file permission. With it, the app can open only the spreadsheets you pick in Google's file picker or that you create from the app. It cannot see or change anything else in your Google Drive.

The app uses this access only to add your results to the spreadsheet you chose, read them back to show your saved results, and set up a Results tab with column headers. The sign-in token stays in your browser for the session and is not sent anywhere except Google.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store or receive your data or your candidates' data on any server.
  • We don't sell or share data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.

Other services the page uses

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, the Excel tools from jsDelivr, and, when you sign in, Google's sign-in and file picker. These services receive ordinary web requests (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your spreadsheets and files stay yours and are not affected.

Contact
